# Prompt 06 — Descriptive Trade-Orientation Results

Generate stable descriptive results only after the coverage audit and source reconciliation are complete.

## Required tables

- exports and imports by colonial, EFTA, EEC, and rest-of-world groups;
- annual group shares;
- absolute and relative changes between 1962 and 1973;
- product composition by destination group;
- concentration indices by destination and product;
- contribution of each group to export growth;
- missingness and source-quality indicators alongside every table.

## Rules

- Use nominal values only for shares within the same year unless a documented deflator is applied.
- Do not interpret association as causality.
- Write results to CSV and concise methodological notes to TXT.
- Do not write paper prose.
