# Prompt 07 — Empirical Extension After Data Stabilisation

Prepare, but do not yet claim, a transparent empirical comparison of colonial and European external demand.

## Minimum prerequisites

- reviewed bilateral trade panel;
- stable product classification;
- Portuguese sectoral output data;
- documented product-to-industry mapping;
- sufficient observations and variation;
- explicit treatment of simultaneity and common European growth shocks.

## Candidate models

- shift-share exposure measures;
- sector and year fixed-effects panels;
- tariff-exposure designs based on documented EFTA schedules;
- colonial commodity-income demand shifters, with direct-price channels controlled.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no aggregate regression with fourteen annual observations presented as causal;
- no machine-learning prediction model;
- no gross-export-to-GDP equivalence;
- no single preferred specification without sensitivity analysis.

## Deliverables

- design matrix CSVs;
- model specification registry;
- diagnostics and robustness CSVs;
- coefficient tables in CSV/TXT;
- assumptions and identification risks in TXT.
