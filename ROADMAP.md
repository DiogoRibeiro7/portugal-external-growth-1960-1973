# Roadmap

What remains between the current repository state and a research-ready empirical panel. Every item
names the readiness requirement it unblocks, so progress can be checked against
`results/live/empirical_readiness_audit.csv` rather than against this prose.

Status at last update: **3 of 35** audit requirements satisfied, **0 of 6** empirical prerequisites,
declaration `NOT_READY`.

## Where the project stands

The historical trade evidence is complete for the period. All fourteen INE yearbooks for 1960-1973
are registered with checksums and transcribed by double entry, giving 599 verified rows covering
World and colonial aggregates, the eight overseas territories, and the European partner rows. Three
requirements are satisfied for the first time: `annual_trade_coverage`,
`colonial_partner_completeness` and `european_partner_completeness`, each 24 of 24.

The constant-composition European benchmark runs annually from 1962 to 1973. Exchange rates are
registered per year from the IMF, so the INE-Comtrade comparison is adjudicated across all twelve
sample years.

What is missing is no longer the trade panel. It is the outcome variable, the product-level
detail, and three decisions.

## 1. Decisions awaiting the author

These need no new data. Each is a research judgement deliberately left open.

### 1.1 Territorial definition status

`config/territorial_definitions.yml` records the `un_comtrade_portugal` scope as
`partially_resolved`. Only the literal value `resolved` satisfies `territorial_consistency`
(currently 0 of 24).

The evidence assembled for the decision:

- INE states the reporting scope on the title page of the 1966-1973 volumes, in both languages:
  *Continente e Ilhas Adjacentes* / *Continent et Iles Adjacentes*.
- Across all fourteen transcribed years the overseas territories appear only as partner rows, never
  inside the reporting territory.
- INE and Comtrade world totals agree to better than 0.03 per cent in 1962-1967 and 1969-1970, and
  to within 1.4 per cent in every year. A territorial mismatch would move the totals by 12 to 25
  per cent, because that is what colonial trade is worth.

The open question is whether inference from agreement settles a question the Comtrade reference
metadata leaves silent. Marking it `resolved` takes `territorial_consistency` from 0 to 24 of 24.

### 1.2 European benchmark composition

The benchmark is eleven countries and covers 1962-1973. The 1960 and 1961 volumes print no separate
Austria row, so no benchmark value is derived for those two years.

- Keep eleven: the series stays twelve years, 1960-61 have no benchmark.
- Drop Austria to ten: the series reaches fourteen years, at the cost of roughly 0.8 per cent of
  trade. On a consistent ten-country basis 1960 is 41.74 per cent and 1962 is 42.72 per cent, so
  the join is smooth.
- Publish both, an eleven-country series for 1962+ and a ten-country variant reaching back to 1960.

Whichever is chosen must be named in the paper as a constant-composition benchmark, not as
"Europe": it excludes Finland and Ireland because the 1962 and 1965 volumes fold them into a
printed residual.

### 1.3 Verification evidence refresh

Freeze checklist items 1-5 read `blocked` because `verification_evidence.csv` attests to a scope
fingerprint that predates this work. The evidence must be regenerated by running the pinned Poetry
toolchain against the current commit; it cannot be written from any other environment without
misrepresenting which tools were run.

## 2. Work that needs no new inputs

### 2.1 Register the CEPII reconciliation scope

`cross_source_reconciliation` is 1 of 2. The TRADHIST subset is committed
(`data/raw/live/cepii/tradhist_portugal_1962_1973.csv`) with the 1.1 GB upstream file kept local and
its SHA-256 recorded.

Building the comparison takes the scope to 2 of 2 and satisfies the requirement. The expected
outcome is `divergence_documented`, not agreement: TRADHIST is denominated in current British
pounds, is largely derived from IMF DOTS mirror flows, and its Portugal totals run 4 to 14 per cent
above INE. That divergence is the finding.

With this and 1.1 done, `reviewed_bilateral_trade_panel` becomes the first satisfied prerequisite.

## 3. Blocked on document acquisition

The route is the one already proven for the INE yearbooks: acquire the volumes, register them with
checksums, transcribe by double entry, reconcile against printed subtotals.

- **EFTA trade statistics** and **OECD Statistics of Foreign Trade** — recorded in
  `config/reconciliation_scopes.yml` as `pending_source_acquisition`. Neither publishes a
  machine-readable historical series for 1962-1973; the current OECD SDMX service carries only
  contemporary datasets. Acquiring either converts its scope to `required` and extends
  cross-source reconciliation beyond two scopes.
- **Overseas statistical yearbooks** (*Anuario Estatistico do Ultramar*) and the **OECD Portugal
  surveys** — 29 of the 43 expected manual source-year records are still missing, and all 29 are
  from these two series rather than from INE.

## 4. Blocked on external access

- **Product-level Comtrade extraction** requires `COMTRADE_SUBSCRIPTION_KEY`. Until then
  `product_level_coverage` stays at 0 of 1, and with it `stable_product_classification`.
- **Product-to-industry mapping** depends on that extraction. `product_to_industry_mapping_coverage`
  is 0 of 1, blocking `documented_product_to_industry_mapping`.

## 5. Blocked on a source that does not yet exist here

The dependent variable has no source at all. `sectoral_output_source_registry` is 0 of 1, and every
downstream sectoral-output requirement is blocked behind it: real output, output growth, deflators,
outcome provenance, and `dependent_variable_coverage` (0 of 88).

A first genuine sectoral-output source — INE industrial production or national accounts by branch
for 1962-1973 — is the single largest remaining gap. The machinery to receive it is built and
tested: source registry with checksum and path containment, sector-year uniqueness, real-output
method validation, source-transition gating, and materialised growth lineage.

Until such a source exists, no empirical design matrix can be built, so
`sufficient_observations_and_variation` and the two model families that depend on
`sectoral_output_growth` remain blocked by construction.

## 6. Manuscript

No manuscript lives in this repository, and freeze checklist item 13 enforces that. The descriptive
evidence now supports writing the non-results sections and a provisional descriptive result around
three exhaustive groups: colonial markets, the constant-composition European benchmark, and residual
destinations. That prose belongs outside this repository.

## Suggested order

1. The three decisions in section 1 — they cost nothing and unblock a prerequisite between them.
2. Register the CEPII scope (2.1).
3. Acquire a sectoral-output source (5). It is the longest pole and everything empirical waits on it.
4. Product-level trade (4), once a Comtrade key is available.
5. EFTA and OECD volumes (3), which strengthen reconciliation but block nothing that the other
   items do not already block.
