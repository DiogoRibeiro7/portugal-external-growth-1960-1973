Portugal territorial-definition review, 1962-1973
=================================================

Status: partially_resolved.

UN Comtrade reporter code 620 is officially labelled Portugal in the public
Reporters.json reference endpoint, with ISO alpha-3 PRT and isGroup false. The
reference metadata does not state whether 1962-1973 observations cover mainland
Portugal only, mainland plus adjacent islands, metropolitan Portugal, Portugal
plus any overseas territories, or another customs/statistical area.

INE 1962 evidence confirms that the Portuguese source uses explicit statistical
territory tables. Volume I page 23 lists Ultramar Portugues before foreign
countries, and pages 38-39 print Ultramar aggregates in Quadro III. Pages 5-6
also identify special treatment for Azores and Madeira and point to tables
XII-XVII.

Research-readiness implication: keep the Comtrade reporter-territory block open
until UN/OECD/Banco de Portugal/EFTA evidence explicitly defines reporter code
620 for the benchmark period.
