Missing colonial partners diagnostic, 1962
==========================================

Status: unresolved; residual calculated but not allocated.

The 1962 INE source provides a complete Ultramar aggregate in Quadro III:
imports on Volume I PDF page 38 and exports on PDF page 39. The source-specific
Ultramar list on PDF page 23 contains eight rows: Cabo Verde, Guine,
S. Tome e Principe, Angola, Mocambique, India, Macau, and Timor.

The current UN Comtrade S1 snapshot for Portugal reporter 620 returns only four
configured colonial partner rows in 1962. Cabo Verde, Macao, and Sao Tome and
Principe were requested but not returned. Portuguese India appears in the INE
Ultramar list, but no separate Comtrade area has been registered for that
source-specific historical entity in this repository.

The INE-minus-observed-Comtrade residual is preserved in
missing_colonies_1962_diagnostic.csv. It is not allocated among missing
territories because the source evidence does not justify allocation and because
partner-code comparability remains unresolved.

No missing colonial observation has been converted to zero.
